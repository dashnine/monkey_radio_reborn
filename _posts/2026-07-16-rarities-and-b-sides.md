**33 new tracks** have joined the Monkey Radio Archive station...and the hunt is nearly over.
{:.lead}

<!--more-->

Most of this batch is rarities and b-sides, the stuff that takes the longest to run down. Highlights include:

* DJ Krush
* Lamb
* Mez
* Boards of Canada (tracks from the Japanese release of _Geogaddi_ )

With this addition, only **8 known tracks** remain to be added...plus the three mystery-tracks that continue to defeat every search (see [Most Wanted](/most-wanted/)).

## One step back: K&D's "1995"

Not all the news is additive. After a review of the available evidence (including the newly rediscovered 2005 tracklist) Kruder & Dorfmeister's _1995_ has been **disabled in the radio rotation** due to insufficient evidence of play.

As covered in [the review](/blog/reviews/2026-06-17-1995/) and [The Sources](/projects/the-sources/), the album's provenance was always suspect: released in 2020 (years after the station went dark) and attributed only via the fan-made Spotify playlist. It's great music, and it absolutely fits the vibe, but with its release date and not a single track appearing on any canonical tracklist it's going on the "probably wasn't there" list for now. If evidence ever surfaces, it'll be back in a heartbeat.

Enjoy!
